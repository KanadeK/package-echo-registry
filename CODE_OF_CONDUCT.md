# Code of Conduct

Package Echo Registry is a technical community where participation must remain safe and useful.

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate with respect and focus criticism on code, evidence, and outcomes;
- welcome questions and different experience levels;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized conduct;
- respect privacy and coordinated security disclosure.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior threatens the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository owner. Reports will be handled confidentially and without retaliation.
